What we fix

Cosmetic auto repair, done at your curb.

Bruno's specializes in the paint and cosmetic work that makes a car look right again — color-matched, blended and finished on-site, so you never lose your car to a shop for days.

Auto Paint & Refinishing

Panel painting and refinishing with color-matched, professionally sprayed coats — for a finish that blends into the rest of the car.

Scratch & Scuff Repair

Keyed doors, parking-lot scuffs and deep scratches sanded, filled and repainted until they simply disappear.

Clearcoat & Paint Restoration

Faded, peeling or sun-baked clearcoat cut back, prepped and re-sprayed — bringing dull, oxidized paint back to a wet shine.

Cosmetic Body Repair

Dings, chips and small cosmetic damage repaired and refinished so the car looks new again — no full body-shop bill.

Car Lockout Service

Locked your keys in the car? We come out fast and get you back in safely — no damage to your vehicle.

Mobile — We Come to You

Everything is done where your car already sits — your driveway, garage or job site — on a time that works for you.

Real prep, real results

The results start with the prep.

A great finish isn't sprayed on — it's earned in the prep. This hood came in with clearcoat that had failed and oxidized in the Florida sun. Bruno's cut it back and sanded it flat so the fresh paint would lay down clean and even.

That patience is exactly why customers describe scratches that "simply disappear" and cars that come back looking like new — even the ones whose owners rarely let anyone touch their vehicle.
